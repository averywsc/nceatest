## Adding waves

- <Term id="superposition">Superposition</Term>: the displacement where two waves meet is the sum of their individual displacements
- For two sources vibrating in phase, with path difference $\Delta L$:
  - **constructive** interference when $\Delta L = n\lambda$
  - **destructive** interference when $\Delta L = \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\lambda$
  - $n = 0, 1, 2, \dots$

<Tips>

- Always give the path difference as a number of wavelengths, then state the type of interference.

</Tips>
