## Harmonics

- A standing wave forms when a wave and its reflection superpose
- A <Term id="node">node</Term> is a point of no movement. An antinode is a point of maximum movement
- For a string of length $L$ fixed at both ends:
  - wavelength $\lambda_n = \dfrac{2L}{n}$
  - frequency $f_n = \dfrac{nv}{2L}$
  - $n = 1$ is the fundamental, $n = 2$ the second harmonic, and so on

<Tips>

- Count the antinodes to find $n$. Two nodes at the ends and one antinode in the middle is $n = 1$.

</Tips>
