## Force toward the centre

- An object moving in a circle accelerates toward the centre: $a_c = \dfrac{v^2}{r} = r\omega^2$
- The net force toward the centre is $F_c = \dfrac{mv^2}{r}$
- Centripetal force is **not** a new force. It is the name for the net force provided by tension, friction, gravity or a normal force
- If the force disappears, the object moves off in a straight line along the tangent

<Tips>

- Never draw a "centripetal force" arrow on a free-body diagram. Draw the real forces, then say which of them provides the net force toward the centre.

</Tips>
