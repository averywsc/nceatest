## Describing motion in a circle

- Angular velocity is the angle turned per second: $\omega = \dfrac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t}$, measured in rad s$^{-1}$
- One full turn is $2\pi$ radians, so $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$
- Linear speed along the circle: $v = r\omega$
- The speed is constant but the **velocity** is not, because its direction keeps changing

<Tips>

- Always work in radians. Convert revolutions per minute by multiplying by $2\pi / 60$.

</Tips>
